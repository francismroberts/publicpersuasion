How to Approach These Four Days

30% of the grade is already banked — Private Writing went in on Aug. 8. Every lecture in the course is watched except the three delivery ones, which are the first three steps below. What's left is producing, not learning: one script, one video, four days. This page is one linear list — work it top to bottom and don't reassemble the plan from five week pages.

  1. Monday — the three delivery lectures, a 12-minute timed write, and one sentence naming your topic and audience. That sentence is the only real Monday deliverable.
  2. Tuesday — structure, then draft. Pick a shape before you start writing, then draft start to finish in one pass.
  3. Wednesday — read aloud, cut to time, revise words, submit the script, print it. Script due Wed. Aug. 26.
  4. Thursday — mark up, rehearse, record, submit, debrief. Video due Thu. Aug. 27.
Every deadline is two hours earlier than it looks All course deadlines are 5:00 p.m. CT, which is 3:00 p.m. Pacific — two hours earlier than the syllabus number reads if you're thinking in local time. The targets on this page are set to 2:00 p.m. PT to leave an hour of buffer.
Speech length: 5–6 minutes DeSanto confirmed 5–6 minutes at the Aug. 24 live session, which resolves the contradiction between the Part 1 and Part 2 assignment PDFs (5–6 vs. 4–5). Aim to land at 5:15–5:45 — roughly 700–850 words depending on your pace. Verify it with a stopwatch, not a word count.
Why so little reading is on this plan The syllabus says the lectures pinpoint the key portions of the materials. Checked against all sixteen lecture transcripts, that holds for the packets (referenced constantly, and by page number) and for Levy (cited in the Private Writing How-To lecture) — but it is false for Cook, which is never mentioned in any lecture. That's why Cook Ch. 4, Ch. 10, and Ch. 7 are the only readings on this plan: they're the assigned material no lecture has already covered for you.
A note on shared steps Several steps here are the same items as on Weeks 4 and 5 — not copies. Checking one checks the other, in both directions. Cook Ch. 10 and Luntz Ch. 12 are Week 4 backlog items, so doing them here shrinks that backlog too. The same applies to Reset: resetting this page unchecks those shared items on their own week pages.

If Everything Goes Sideways

If the week collapses — work, illness, anything — two things still have to happen, and nothing else on this page does. Cut everything back to these:

  1. A written script uploaded by Wed. 3:00 p.m. PT — 40 points.
  2. A video delivered with conviction by Thu. 3:00 p.m. PT — 30 points.
Everything else is optional relative to those two uploads The readings, the re-skims, the rehearsal passes and the debrief all make the two uploads better. None of them are worth a point on their own. If you're out of time, protect the uploads and drop the rest without guilt.
